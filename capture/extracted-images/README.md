# Images recovered from the PDF exports

`static.wixstatic.com` is unreachable from the build environment, so none of the
site's assets could be fetched. They were extracted from the embedded image
streams in the Safari PDF exports instead.

## The logo — used

`home-223x142-Im3.png` is the real badge, transparency intact. It is in the build
at `public/brand/curbology-logo.png`. The Wix HTML names it
`3f6c3b_122a194aa059428a978250e93b55845a~mv2.png`.

223×142 is the largest raster that exists. There is no vector original, and the
"EXTERIOR SERVICES" bar degrades below about 50px.

## The photography — NOT used, and here is why

Nine photographs came out alongside it. **None of them is wired into the site**,
because none can be verified as Curbology's own work:

- `home-1122x1122-Im1.png` — a worker soft-washing siding. **The wordmark on his
  polo is garbled**, which is the signature of an AI-generated image. It is not a
  photograph of anyone who works at Curbology.
- `home-1314x941-Im6.png`, `svc-*` — the same treatment: plausible, generic,
  unattributable.
- `pf-900x502-Im2..Im7.png` — the six portfolio entries. Generic house exteriors
  with no before/after pairing and nothing identifying a Curbology job.

The house rule is that the photograph is the argument on an exterior-cleaning
site — "this is what your house will look like". An AI-generated worker in an
invented uniform is a worse claim than an empty plate, because it is a claim that
is false. The site ships honest empty states until Tristen supplies real job
photos.

They are kept here as evidence of what the old site shipped, not as assets.
